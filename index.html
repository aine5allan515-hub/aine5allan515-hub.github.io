02 / The person behind the stories

Hello, I'm
Allan.

I'm a storyteller and communications assistant based in Kampala, Uganda. I work in public relations at Makerere University's College of Humanities and Social Sciences (CHUSS), while pursuing a Bachelor of Journalism and Communication.

My work brings together writing, photography, video, design and social media. I'm interested in finding the human story in big ideas and making it clear, engaging and meaningful.
